## The Challenge
The recovery journey from Substance Abuse Disorder (SUD) is often lonely and prone to relapse without a strong support system. Collaborative research with UMass Dartmouth highlighted the need for a non-stigmatizing, mobile-first support platform.

## The Solution
**ReLeafApp** is a comprehensive recovery companion that bridges the gap between clinical support and daily life.

### Features
- **Social Support Grid**: A secure, private network for users to connect with peers and mentors.
- **Mood & Recovery Tracking**: Interactive journals and progress visualizations to reinforce positive behavior.
- **Crisis Mapping**: Geo-tagged resources (meetings, clinics, support centers) available on-demand.
- **Cross-Platform**: Built once with Flutter to serve both Android and Web users seamlessly.

## Impact
- **Institutional Deployment**: Adopted as a research tool for community health studies at UMass.
- **Scalable Architecture**: Flexible Firebase backend allows for rapid feature iteration based on clinical feedback.
- **Accessibility Focus**: Designed with Portuguese and Spanish localization to serve diverse community needs.
